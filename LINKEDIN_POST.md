### 💡 What is DarkPattern Guard?

DarkPattern Guard empowers shoppers to audit suspicious checkouts and expose manipulative web interfaces before authorizing payments:

🔍 **Multimodal AI Inspector**:
Drop in a checkout screenshot or paste a suspicious site URL. Powered by **Google Gemini 1.5 Flash** (via the official `@google/genai` SDK), our AI analyzes the visual hierarchy and checkout copy, extracts strict JSON audits, and scores deceptive risk from 0 to 100.

🚦 **Severity Rating & User Safety Tips**:
Categorizes patterns into **Red for High Risk (≥70)** and **Yellow for Medium Risk**, accompanied by itemized deception bullet points and actionable consumer countermeasures.

🗳️ **Community Wall of Shame**:
A live crowdsourced feed where users report unethical layouts, browse by category (Pre-checked Boxes, Drip Pricing, Urgency Traps, Roach Motel), filter by domain search bar, and cast real-time Upvotes & Downvotes.

📚 **Interactive Dark Pattern Field Guide**:
An educational glossary teaching consumers how to spot the 7 major dark patterns in seconds and defend their wallets.

### 🛠️ Built with a 100% Free & Scalable Stack:
- **AI Core**: Google Gemini 1.5 Flash via `@google/genai` (Google AI Studio Free Tier)
- **Frontend**: Next.js 15 (App Router) + React 19 + TypeScript
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS with custom glassmorphism & dark cyber-defense aesthetics
- **Icons**: Lucide-React
- **Database**: Zero-cost persistent local JSON storage with in-memory caching
